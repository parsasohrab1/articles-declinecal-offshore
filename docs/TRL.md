# TRL assessment (evidence-based) – DeclineCal

**Level reached: TRL 4 for the component, on SYNTHETIC wells only.** TRL 5 is not claimed.

`pytest` (5 tests) passes on 2026-11-04:

| Component | Module | Validated against |
|---|---|---|
| Arps fit | `arps.py` | recovery of known noise-free parameters; reproduces the curve within 2% |
| Per-lead conformal bounds | `conformal.py` | coverage between 0.85 and 0.95 for a nominal 0.90 on exchangeable synthetic wells; bounds widen with the lead month; refusal with fewer than 20 calibration wells |
| Behaviour under a regime change | `conformal.coverage` | coverage falls by more than 0.05 when the rate drops after the fitting window (the guarantee does not hold, as expected) |

## Not done
- **No real production data.** The synthetic wells are generated by an Arps model plus log-normal noise, so the 0.90 coverage shows that the code does what it is designed to do, not how it performs on a field.
- No comparison with a baseline band or with other decline models (exponential, Duong), no cumulative-production forecast, no handling of shut-ins.

## Next steps toward TRL 5
Monthly rates of at least 100 real wells of one field (for example public Volve data), the measured coverage on held-out wells and a comparison with a fixed percentage band.
